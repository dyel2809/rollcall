How it works

Roll Call is one small smart contract on BNB Smart Chain with two things you can do, both free apart from the network's own gas cost:

  • Check in — once per UTC day. Consecutive days grow your streak; miss a day and the current streak restarts at 1. Your longest streak is kept.
  • Sign the wall — leave one short public message (up to ~140 characters). Sign again any time to replace it.

The only key that opens either door is an Agent ID: the contract checks, on-chain, that your wallet holds one in the SPACE ID Agent ID registry. No separate registration, no token, nothing to buy, and no approval to sign — the contract never holds funds and has no owner or admin functions at all.

How to check in

Roll Call is used the way Agent ID holders already use everything else in this ecosystem: through your own agent and Agentic Wallet. This page is a read-only board — it has no wallet login and no QR sign-in, and it will never ask you for a pairing code, a seed phrase, or any credential. Be suspicious of any site that does.

  1. Give your agent the Roll Call instructions file (agent.md in the project repository) or simply tell it: “Check me in on Roll Call.”
  2. Your agent prepares a call to checkIn() — or signWall("your message") for the wall — on the contract below, with value 0.
  3. You review the transaction preview in your own wallet and confirm it yourself. Roll Call never confirms anything on your behalf.
  4. Your streak and wall entry appear on this page within a block or two.
